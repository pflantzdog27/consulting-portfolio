# About Adam Pflantzer

## Principal Consultant & Founder

Adam Pflantzer is the founder and principal consultant at Pflantzer Consulting, bringing nearly a decade of specialized experience in ServiceNow user experience and interface design.

## Background

Adam's journey into the ServiceNow ecosystem began almost 10 years ago, and he quickly found his niche at the intersection of technology and design. While many consultants focused purely on technical configuration or pure design work, Adam recognized the need for professionals who could bridge both worlds—understanding the technical constraints of the ServiceNow platform while championing exceptional user experience.

### Professional Experience

Throughout his career, Adam has worked across diverse organizational contexts:

- **Big Four Consulting**: Gained experience at KPMG working on large-scale enterprise implementations, learning to navigate complex organizational structures and deliver within rigorous quality standards
- **Specialized ServiceNow Consultancies**: Deepened technical expertise and refined ServiceNow-specific best practices at firms dedicated exclusively to the platform
- **Cross-Industry Exposure**: Worked with clients across multiple industries, developing a versatile approach that adapts to different organizational cultures and user needs

This varied experience provides a well-rounded perspective on what works (and what doesn't) in ServiceNow implementations of all sizes.

- **Virtual Agent**: Designing and implementing conversational interfaces that actually help users

- **Interface Design**: Creating intuitive, accessible interfaces that align with user mental models
