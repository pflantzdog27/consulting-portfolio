# Contact & Communication Policies

### Standard Response Times

- Same business day for all inquiries

- AI-powered chatbot provides immediate responses 24/7

**Project Communication**
- Active project queries: Within 4 business hours

**Emergency Support** (for retainer clients)

- **Primary Availability:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM Eastern Time

## Meeting Types & Formats

### Discovery & Consultation Meetings

**Initial Consultation Call**
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Cost:** Free, no obligation
- **Format:** Video call (Zoom, Teams, or phone)
- **Purpose:** Understand your needs, discuss potential solutions, determine fit
- **Scheduling:** Available within 1-2 business days of request

**Discovery Workshop**
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours (or multiple shorter sessions)
- **Cost:** Included in project pricing
- **Format:** Video call preferred, can be onsite
- **Purpose:** Deep dive into requirements, user research, process mapping
- **Participants:** Key stakeholders, end users, technical team members

---

### Project Meetings

**Kickoff Meeting**
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Format:** Video call
- **Purpose:** Align on goals, timeline, roles, communication plan
- **Attendees:** Project team, stakeholders, decision makers

**Regular Status Calls**
- **Frequency:** Weekly or bi-weekly (based on project pace)
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Format:** Video call
- **Purpose:** Progress updates, demos, issue resolution, next steps
- **Scheduling:** Recurring day/time agreed at kickoff

**Design Review Sessions**
- **Duration:** 60-90 minutes
- **Format:** Video call with screen sharing
- **Purpose:** Review wireframes, mockups, prototypes; gather feedback
- **Participants:** Design stakeholders, key users if beneficial

**UAT & Demo Sessions**
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Format:** Video call with live system access
- **Purpose:** User acceptance testing, feature demonstrations, feedback gathering
- **Frequency:** As needed during testing phase

---

### Support & Maintenance Meetings

**Monthly Check-In** (for retainer clients)
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Format:** Video or phone call
- **Purpose:** Review utilization, discuss upcoming needs, address any concerns
- **Scheduling:** First week of each month

**Quarterly Business Review** (for larger retainer clients)
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Format:** Video call with presentation
- **Purpose:** Review metrics, discuss strategic roadmap, plan enhancements
- **Scheduling:** Once per quarter

---

### Training Sessions

**End User Training**
- **Duration:** 60-90 minutes per session
- **Format:** Video call or onsite
- **Size:** Up to 20 participants per session
- **Purpose:** Teach employees how to use new portal/features

**Technical Training/Workshops**
- **Duration:** Half-day (4 hours) or full-day (8 hours)
- **Format:** Virtual or onsite
- **Size:** Up to 10 participants for hands-on workshops
- **Purpose:** Build internal team capability

## Scheduling Guidelines

### How to Schedule Meetings

1. **Initial Consultation:** Use website contact form or email
2. **Project Meetings:** Calendar links provided at kickoff, or coordinate via email
3. **Ad-Hoc Meetings:** Email with 2-3 time options, or request calendar link

### Notice Requirements

- **Standard meetings:** 24 hours notice preferred
- **Urgent meetings:** Best effort same-day if availability permits
- **Onsite meetings:** 2+ weeks advance notice
- **Large workshops:** 3+ weeks advance notice

### Cancellation Policy

- **More than 24 hours notice:** No charge, easy rescheduling
- **Less than 24 hours notice:** No charge for first occurrence; repeated late cancellations may result in meeting fee
- **No-show:** May result in meeting fee (50% of scheduled time at hourly rate)

*Note: We understand emergencies happen and apply this policy reasonably*

## Communication Best Practices

### For Fastest Response

1. **Be specific:** "Need help with catalog item approval workflow" vs. "Have a question"
2. **Include context:** Relevant screenshots, error messages, or examples
3. **Indicate urgency:** Label urgent items clearly
4. **Use appropriate channel:** Emergency = phone, routine = email

### During Projects

- Check in regularly at scheduled meetings
- Raise concerns early rather than waiting
- Provide feedback promptly on deliverables
- Keep us informed of changing priorities

### For Support Requests

- Describe the issue and impact
- Include steps to reproduce if applicable
- Note if users are currently affected
- Specify expected vs. actual behavior
