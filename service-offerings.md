# Service Offerings

## Overview

Pflantzer Consulting provides comprehensive ServiceNow UX/UI services across the entire project lifecycle—from initial discovery and design through implementation, training, and ongoing support. We specialize in Employee Center, Service Portal, custom applications, Virtual Agent, and Gen AI implementations.

## Core Service Areas

### Employee Center Implementation

Transform your ServiceNow instance with a modern, consumer-grade user interface that employees actually want to use.

**What We Do:**
- Design and implement Employee Center from scratch or migrate from Service Portal
- Create custom workspaces and landing pages tailored to different user personas
- Build intuitive navigation structures and search experiences
- Integrate AI-powered assistance and recommendations
- Optimize mobile experience and responsive design
- Ensure accessibility compliance (WCAG standards)

**Best For:** Organizations looking to modernize their ServiceNow user interface, improve adoption rates, or replace outdated Service Portal implementations.

---

### Service Portal Development

Expert development and customization of ServiceNow's Angular-based portal framework for legacy systems or specific use cases where Service Portal remains the right choice.

**What We Do:**
- Build custom Service Portal themes aligned with your brand
- Develop custom widgets for unique functionality
- Create role-based portal experiences for different user groups
- Optimize portal performance and load times
- Implement advanced search and filtering capabilities
- Migrate content and configurations between instances

**Best For:** Organizations with existing Service Portal investments, complex custom requirements, or specific technical needs that Employee Center doesn't yet address.

---

### Custom Application Development

Build bespoke applications within ServiceNow that solve unique business problems with intuitive, user-friendly interfaces.

**What We Do:**
- Design and develop custom ServiceNow applications
- Create tailored workflows and approval processes
- Build custom UI components and interfaces
- Integrate with external systems and data sources
- Develop mobile-friendly experiences
- Implement role-based access and security models

**Best For:** Organizations with unique business processes that require custom solutions beyond out-of-the-box ServiceNow functionality.

---

### Virtual Agent & Conversational UI

Design and implement AI-powered chatbots and virtual agents that provide genuine value rather than frustration.

**What We Do:**
- Design conversational flows and dialogue structures
- Implement Virtual Agent topics and integrations
- Create natural language understanding (NLU) models
- Build escalation paths to live agents when needed
- Optimize bot performance based on usage analytics
- Integrate with NowAssist and other AI capabilities

**Best For:** Organizations looking to deflect common service requests, provide 24/7 support, or improve self-service adoption through conversational interfaces.

---

### Gen AI Integration (NowAssist, AI Agent)

Leverage ServiceNow's latest AI capabilities to enhance user productivity and experience.

**What We Do:**
- Implement NowAssist for intelligent search and suggestions
- Configure AI Agent for automated request handling
- Design AI-powered knowledge recommendations
- Create intelligent routing and assignment workflows
- Optimize AI models based on your organizational data
- Train teams on effective AI feature usage

**Best For:** Organizations ready to leverage cutting-edge AI capabilities to improve efficiency, reduce manual work, and provide smarter user experiences.
