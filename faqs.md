# Frequently Asked Questions

Think of us as the team that makes ServiceNow actually enjoyable to use, not just functional. We're unique because we combine both—nearly a decade of hands-on ServiceNow development experience with genuine UX expertise. Plus, as a lean consultancy, we're significantly more affordable than Big Four firms while maintaining premium quality.

### Do you only work with large enterprises?

Not at all. We work with organizations of all sizes—from mid-market companies implementing ServiceNow for the first time to large enterprises optimizing existing implementations. Our flexible engagement models and competitive pricing make us accessible to companies that might assume they can't afford specialized UX expertise.

### Is Pflantzer Consulting an official ServiceNow partner?

We're working toward official ServiceNow partnership status as we grow. In the meantime, our certifications, experience, and track record demonstrate our expertise. We stay current with the latest ServiceNow releases and maintain the same quality standards as established partners.

## Service & Process Questions

### What does a typical engagement look like?

Most engagements follow this pattern:

1. **Discovery** (1-2 weeks): We conduct workshops, user interviews, and requirements gathering to understand your needs
2. **Design** (1-2 weeks): We create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes for your approval
3. **Development** (2-8 weeks): We build and configure your solution iteratively with regular check-ins
4. **Testing** (1-2 weeks): UAT, bug fixes, and refinements based on feedback
5. **Deployment** (1 week): Go-live support and knowledge transfer
6. **Post-Launch** (ongoing): Optional support and optimization

Timelines vary based on project scope and complexity.

### How involved do we need to be in the project?

We need your involvement during key phases—especially discovery (to share requirements), design review (to approve direction), and UAT (to validate solutions). The level of day-to-day involvement is flexible based on your preferences. Some clients want weekly check-ins, others prefer more autonomy with milestone reviews. We adapt to what works for you.

### Can you work with our existing ServiceNow team?

Absolutely! We often work alongside internal ServiceNow teams, either filling specific UX/UI gaps or handling entire portal/application builds while your team focuses on other priorities. We're collaborative by nature and happy to mentor your team on UX best practices.

### Do you provide training for our internal team?

Yes! We offer both formal training workshops and informal knowledge transfer during implementations. We can train your team on Service Portal development, Employee Center customization, UX design principles, or Virtual Agent implementation—whatever helps you build internal capability.

### What if we need help after the project ends?

We offer flexible ongoing support options including monthly retainers, ad-hoc hourly support, or project-based enhancements. Many clients start with a retainer for the first few months post-launch, then transition to as-needed support once things stabilize.

Pricing varies based on project scope, but here are our typical ranges:

- **Hourly Rate:** $70-100/hour (depending on project complexity)
- **Small Projects:** $5,000-$15,000 (portal enhancements, custom widgets)

We're significantly less expensive than Big Four firms (often 40-60% less) while delivering equivalent or better quality.

### How does your pricing compare to larger consultancies?

Big Four consultancies typically charge $150-300+ per hour for ServiceNow work. Specialized ServiceNow partners often charge $125-200+ per hour. Our rates of $70-100/hour represent substantial savings without compromising on quality—in fact, you get direct access to a senior consultant rather than a mixed team of varying experience levels.

### Do you offer fixed-price projects?

Yes, for well-defined projects with clear requirements. After our discovery phase, we can provide fixed pricing for specific deliverables. This works well for implementations, migrations, or specific feature builds. For ongoing optimization or projects with evolving requirements, time & materials or retainer models work better.

### What's included in your hourly rate?

Everything: design, development, testing, documentation, project management, and communication. No surprise fees or add-ons. Travel expenses for onsite work would be billed separately if applicable.

### Do you require a large upfront payment?

No. We typically structure payments as:
- Small projects: 50% upfront, 50% on completion
- Large projects: Milestone-based (often 30% upfront, 40% at midpoint, 30% at completion)
- Retainers: Monthly billing

We're flexible and can discuss payment terms that work for your procurement process.

## Technical Questions

### Which ServiceNow modules do you work with?

Our primary focus is the UX/UI layer, which can touch any module. We have deep experience with:

- **IT Service Management (ITSM)**: Incident, request, knowledge, CMDB
- **Customer Service Management (CSM)**: Case management, customer portals
- **HR Service Delivery (HRSD)**: Employee services, HR request portals
- **Source-to-Pay & Procurement**: Supplier portals, procurement requests
- **Finance Operations**: Financial service delivery

We work with these modules in the context of building portals, Employee Centers, and custom applications.

### Do you only work on Employee Center, or Service Portal too?

Both! While Employee Center is the modern direction for ServiceNow, many organizations still rely on Service Portal or have specific use cases where Service Portal remains appropriate. We're experts in both platforms and can advise on which makes sense for your situation.

### Can you build custom integrations?

We can handle standard ServiceNow integrations using REST APIs and IntegrationHub basics. For complex enterprise integration architecture or middleware solutions, we may recommend partnering with integration specialists. Our strength is in the front-end experience, not backend integration complexity.

### Do you work with ServiceNow's AI features?

Yes! We implement and optimize NowAssist, AI Agent, Virtual Agent, and other Gen AI capabilities. We're particularly skilled at designing AI-powered experiences that enhance rather than complicate the user experience.

### What if we're on an older ServiceNow version?

We can work with most ServiceNow versions, though we'll recommend upgrading if your version is significantly outdated or lacks critical features needed for your project. We can also assist with upgrade planning and testing.

### Can you help with mobile experiences?

Absolutely. Both Employee Center and Service Portal support responsive design, and we ensure all our implementations work seamlessly on mobile devices. We can also work with the ServiceNow mobile app if that's part of your strategy.
