# Company History

## Overview

Pflantzer Consulting is a ServiceNow consulting firm specializing in user experience and interface design for ServiceNow platforms. Founded by Adam Pflantzer in January 2026, the company bridges a critical gap in the ServiceNow consulting market by combining technical platform expertise with exceptional UX/UI design capabilities.

## Our Story

After nearly a decade of working in the ServiceNow ecosystem—including experience at Big Four consulting firms like KPMG and specialized ServiceNow consultancies—Adam Pflantzer identified a consistent problem: many consultancies could build technically sound ServiceNow solutions, but they often lacked the UX/UI expertise to make those solutions truly user-friendly and adoptable.

Too often, organizations invest significant resources in ServiceNow implementations only to face low adoption rates because the interfaces are confusing, unintuitive, or fail to meet end-users' needs. Pflantzer Consulting was created to solve this problem by delivering ServiceNow solutions that are both technically robust and delightfully easy to use.

## What Makes Us Different

Unlike traditional ServiceNow consultancies that focus primarily on technical configuration and implementation, Pflantzer Consulting brings a design-first approach to every engagement. We believe that technology is only valuable when people can actually use it—and use it well.

Our unique combination of skills includes:

- **Deep ServiceNow Technical Expertise**: Nearly 10 years of hands-on experience with Employee Center, Service Portal, Virtual Agent, and custom application development
- **User Experience Design**: Professional UX research, workshop facilitation, and design methodologies applied specifically to ServiceNow
- **AI Integration**: Expertise in implementing and optimizing ServiceNow's Gen AI capabilities, including NowAssist and AI Agent
- **Full-Stack Delivery**: From discovery workshops to implementation to ongoing support, we handle the complete lifecycle

## Our Growth

While Pflantzer Consulting currently operates as a lean, agile solo consultancy, this structure allows us to:

- Provide personalized, hands-on service directly from an experienced expert
- Maintain lower overhead and pass savings directly to clients
- Make quick decisions and pivot rapidly to meet client needs
- Ensure consistency and quality across all deliverables

As we grow, we remain committed to maintaining the personalized service and quality that sets us apart, while strategically expanding our capabilities to serve more clients.

## Looking Forward

Our vision is to become the go-to ServiceNow partner for organizations that understand that great technology requires great design. We're building a future where ServiceNow implementations aren't just functional—they're experiences that users genuinely enjoy and that drive real business outcomes through high adoption and efficient processes.
